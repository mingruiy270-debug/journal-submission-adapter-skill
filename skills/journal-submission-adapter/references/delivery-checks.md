# Final Review And Minimal Submission Package

## Before Formatting

- Journal, article type and submission stage have been verified on official pages.
- Comparator papers have verified identities/dates, a recorded access route (local full text or a browser-readable fallback), and honest reading coverage. Selected-paper counts are not completed-reading counts.
- Adaptation choices refer to actual reading observations, not assumed house style.
- The main claim is clear; each major result serves a scientific question.
- Measurements and model outputs are named accurately; sample/effective n and testing families are consistent.
- Author facts and scientific numbers were not invented to fill missing sections.

## After Formatting

Read every final uploaded file's relevant content, not just its filename or a passing script. For spreadsheets check sheet names, headings, source identifiers, representative rows and core numerical records. For images inspect labels, axes, statistics, legends, scale bars and readability at the intended display size. For PDFs inspect page count, visible symbols, titles and figure/legend placement.

Compare manuscript, abstract, cover letter, figures, legends, tables and supplements for:

- Same study design, group definitions, n, unit of analysis, effect definition and statistical family.
- Consistent gene/cell-state terminology and programme direction.
- Correct current figure/table numbering and existing destinations for all callouts.
- Preserved citation fields and correct bibliography, with Refresh when applicable.
- Required declarations grounded in author-supplied information.
- No obsolete journal names, debug notes, placeholder stars, TODO/TBD, internal filenames in reader-facing prose or missing attachment references.

Do not blanket-delete scientific terms that also occur in working notes, such as frozen tissue, validation cohort or quality control. Inspect meaning. A numerical inconsistency is not solved by removing its explanation.

## Upload Decisions

Classify the official requirements explicitly:

| Status | Upload action |
| --- | --- |
| Mandatory at initial submission | Include, or report a real blocker if missing |
| Conditional and applicable | Include |
| Optional but needed to support/cite a reported result | Include a suitable accessible result file or revise the claim/callout transparently |
| Optional and unnecessary | Omit in a minimal package |
| Production/revision only | Defer until that stage |
| Requirement unavailable or ambiguous | Identify the question; do not label as verified |

Do not impose raw-data uploads just because another journal wanted them. Equally, an author's preference for fewer files does not cancel a data policy or permit a broken reference to a removed supplement. Private review links/tokens belong only in the intended confidential channel, not public files.

Keep `upload/` free of reading cards, download PDFs, scripts, audit logs and intermediate manuscript versions. A useful working area contains current requirements, selected-paper records, reading cards, a short adaptation map and final review findings. Do not create elaborate evidence ledgers or hash lists for their own sake.

## Completion Statement

Report concrete final paths and concise changes. State which policies and comparator papers were used and whether full texts were read. Separate successful checks from untested ones. List mandatory missing information and unresolved scientific contradictions plainly; do not bury them in optional suggestions. A task may be complete as preparation even when author facts or an external portal preview remain to be checked, but must not be called unconditionally ready to submit.
